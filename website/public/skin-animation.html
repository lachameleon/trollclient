faq

can other people really see it?

Yes. Which skin layers you show is part of your client settings, and the server passes it on to every player who can see you. That's why it works on any server, and why nobody needs a mod to see it.

will it mess up my skin settings?

No. SkinBlink remembers which layers you had on when it starts and puts them back when you switch it off, when the trigger stops, and when you leave a world, so your options file never saves a half-blinked skin.

how many packets is that?

One per change. At the fastest setting (1 tick) that's 20 a second; at the default of 3 ticks, about 7. If a server complains, turn the interval up.

my skin doesn't have an outer layer

Then only your cape has anything to flicker. Skins with a hat, jacket, sleeves and trousers layer look best. Load yours in the lab above to see what you're working with.

where is it in the client?

Press Right Shift, open the Player tab, left click SkinBlink to switch it on and right click it for these settings. Or type .toggle skinblink in chat.
