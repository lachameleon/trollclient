all the modules

Every module in the client, read straight out of the source code. In game: left click toggles, right click opens settings, middle click binds a key. Hover a setting name to see what it does, or try the menu right here in your browser.

combat 5 · keep the crystals and arrows off you
CrystalCancelWalls off every crystal other players place using the cheapest block in your hotbar.
settings: range, delay mode, delay, min delay, max delay, blocks, ignore own, require enemy, rotate, swap back, air place
ArrowDodgeStrafes out of the way of anyone aiming at you, without walking off your pillar.
settings: range, detect aim, aim tolerance, detect arrows, all projectiles, lookahead, crouch, jump, safe edges, commit, ignore friends
PopCounterCounts totem pops for every player nearby and announces them.
settings: range, notify, announce, pop message, announce deaths, death message, count self, ignore friends
TrapperEncases a standing-still player in your cheapest blocks: walls, head and lid.
settings: target, shape, only when still, still for, blocks per tick, delay, rotate, air place, swap back, ignore friends
GrudgeWhoever hits you becomes the target of every module with a Target, until it wears off.
settings: retarget, payback, forget after, settle on death, announce, message, projectiles, ignore friends
movement 5 · go places, or get away from them
PlayerAvoidRuns away from every player within a radius using a threat-aware pathfinder.
settings: radius, flee distance, sprint, face movement, max drop, recalculate, ignore friends
PlayerFollowFollows a player of your choice with pathfinding, keeping a set distance.
settings: target, distance, movement, sprint, face movement, stare, recalculate
OrbitWalks circles around a player at a set radius, turning back at ledges.
settings: target, range, radius, direction, switch every, face target, hop, sprint, ignore friends
StalkerCreeps along behind a player out of their view. Angel mode freezes whenever they look.
settings: target, mode, range, distance, their fov, sneak, stare, ignore friends
GoalieGets in front of a player and stays there, matching every sidestep. Good luck getting past.
settings: target, range, distance, block, stare, jump, taunt, ignore friends

TwerkMoves your body in weird ways constantly: turning, shifting, flailing.
settings: mode, shift spam, shift interval, speed, flail arms, sway, show in f5
WindAnnoyThrows wind charges at every player within a radius.
settings: range, delay mode, delay, min delay, max delay, targeting, aim, predict, line of sight, swap back, ignore friends
NoWayHomePuts a block in front of a player in every direction they face.
settings: range, look ahead, wall height, follow pitch, delay, blocks per tick, material, rotate, swap back, air place, ignore friends
FoodAnnoyConstantly starts eating but never finishes, just for the noise.
settings: min bite, max bite, pause, drinks too, prefer offhand, swap back, only when idle
MimicCopies a player's crouching, jumping, swinging, head and steps. Mirror mode is extra creepy.
settings: target, range, sneak, jump, swing, look, walk, ignore friends
MorseSpells a message in Morse code by crouching (or swinging) on the spot.
settings: message, signal, unit, loop, loop gap, only when watched, watch range
TauntSays something rude when nearby players die or pop, then teabags the spot.
settings: radius, on death, on totem pop, chat, death lines, pop lines, chat cooldown, teabag, duration, walk over, ignore friends
RacketSpams doors, trapdoors, gates, levers, bells and note blocks around other players.
settings: doors, trapdoors, fence gates, levers, buttons, bells, note blocks, speed, near players, player radius, rotate, ignore friends
GifterThrows junk from your hotbar at every nearby player. You're welcome.
settings: range, items, delay, keep, line of sight, swap back, ignore friends
PelterThrows snowballs and eggs at nearby players, solving the arc so they hit.
settings: ammo, range, delay, predict, high arc, swap back, ignore friends
ConfettiLaunches firework rockets at players' feet, around you, or to celebrate a kill.
settings: mode, delay, volley, radius, rotate, swap back, ignore friends
GraffitiPlaces signs with your messages right where nearby players will read them.
settings: messages, where, range, delay, stop after, swap back, ignore friends
HonkBlows a goat horn at players who walk up to you (or nonstop). Heard for miles.
settings: trigger, range, hold, face them, swap back, ignore friends

SpammerSends lines from a list on a timer. With an anti-repeat suffix so filters don't catch it.

GreeterWelcomes players who join, says hi (and waves) when they walk up, and bye when they leave.
settings: joins, join lines, leaves, leave lines, walk ups, range, walk up lines, wave, audience, cooldown
AutoReplyAnswers people who say your name or ask a question: Magic 8-Ball, your lines, or "no u".

TypoMisspells a word in your messages, then sends a "*correction" a moment later.
