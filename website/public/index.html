welcome!!

Troll Client is a black & white client for Minecraft 26.2 with a CS:GO-cheat-menu style GUI, a demoscene title screen, skin animation and 35 modules for being mildly annoying.

Press Right Shift in game. Left click a module to turn it on, right click it for settings. That's the whole manual.

features

skin animation

Your outer skin layers blink, wave, strobe or flicker at random. Everyone sees it. Trigger it on jumps, sneaks, hits or always.

the menu

Tabs, LED pips, equalizers on enabled modules, a fake shell prompt, typewriter tooltips, keyboard navigation and middle-click binds.

7 themes

Noir, Paper, Graphite, Terminal, Newsprint, Ink, Fog, or build your own. Scanlines, film grain and dithered shadows included.

5 shaders

Post-processing for the world: film noir, monochrome CRT, 1-bit dither, newspaper halftone and pen & ink.

title screen

Warp starfield, a 3D pixel logo that drops in letter by letter, a sine scroller. Holds its ground against other mods' menus.

GUI tools + macros

Drop or delay container packets, de-sync, save a GUI for later, and chain steps into macros you bind to keys.

HUD

Animated module list, notifications, a sweep radar and a fighting-game target card with a health bar that shakes when they get hit.

35 modules

Twerk, NoWayHome, Parrot, Racket, Honk, Graffiti and friends. All of them mildly annoying, some of them very.

skins

SkinChanger ships five black & white skins and the Troll Client cape (client-side: only you see those). Drag one to spin it. They're blinking because SkinBlink is on.
